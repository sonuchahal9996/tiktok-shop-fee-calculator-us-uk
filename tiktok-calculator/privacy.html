Third-party ad servers or ad networks (including Google AdSense) use technology in the advertisements and links that appear on TokFeeCalc.com. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, or request deletion of consumer data. We do not sell your personal data.
